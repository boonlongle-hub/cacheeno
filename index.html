Cacheeno Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

Cacheeno does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share any personal information. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

This policy applies to the Cacheeno mobile app (package name com.bon.cacheeno), developed by Bon ("I", "me"). Cacheeno is an entertainment app that generates random 0s and 1s. It involves no real money, betting, or gambling.

Information I collect

None. Cacheeno has no accounts or sign-in, and it does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking tools. The app does not send any data from your device to me or to anyone else.

Data stored on your device

To work, Cacheeno saves a small amount of data locally on your phone:

  • Your presets (session lengths and "significantly more" thresholds)
  • Your app settings, such as sound, haptics, and theme
  • Your recent session history and results

This data never leaves your device and I cannot see it. It is not personal information. Results are generated on your device by its built-in random number generator.

Sharing your results

If you tap Share on a result, Cacheeno opens your device's standard share menu with a short text summary. You choose where it goes, such as a messaging app. Cacheeno does not receive or keep a copy, and whatever app you share to handles the text under its own privacy policy.

Permissions and third parties

Cacheeno does not ask for access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or files. It uses your phone's vibration and system sounds only for tap feedback, and you can turn these off in Settings.

Cacheeno contains no third-party SDKs that collect data. If you download it from Google Play, Google may collect information about your download and device under Google's Privacy Policy. I don't control that collection and don't receive any personal data from it.

Children

Cacheeno is intended for adults 18 and older. Because it collects no data, it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your choices

You can delete your presets, history, and settings at any time by clearing Cacheeno's app data in your phone's settings or by uninstalling the app. Since I hold no data about you, there is nothing for me to access, correct, or delete on your behalf.

Changes to this policy

If Cacheeno ever starts collecting data, I will update this policy first and change the effective date at the top. Any changes will be posted on this page.
